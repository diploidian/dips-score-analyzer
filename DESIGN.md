# Design System: dips score

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Race Sheet"**

dips score reads like the sheet a coach hands a driver after a session: one giant result, one fix, and the laps that need a second look marked by hand. Every page is a ruled sheet on a warm ivory or near-black ground, divided by hairlines into modules, each module named by a small boxed tracked-caps label. Exactly one surface per screen is a solid colour plate, and it always holds the thing to change next. The system refuses the equal-weight widget wall of analytics dashboards: sizes are deliberately unequal, so the score numeral at 150px dwarfs everything and the eye has nowhere else to land first.

The world ships as two themes, each with a day and a night mode, all four as complete token sets. **Timeless** is ivory with a dark walnut rail, an olive plate, rust and teal measures, and an amber highlighter that glows at night. **Classical** is soft ivory with a pale rail, a burgundy plate, gold leaf and forest, and a hot-rose highlighter; its night mode is warm near-black with lifted burgundy. Timeless is the default theme. Day or night defaults to Auto, which follows the OS setting; the "Look" group at the foot of the rail holds both toggles, and a choice is remembered. Theme and mode are a first-class part of the system: no colour exists outside the four sets.

Barlow carries body text and Barlow Condensed carries every number, heading and label; Caveat appears only in the hand-drawn marks. Density is high but calm: tabular figures, 1px rules, square corners, no shadows. The shipping app is PyQt6 and the build path (QSS and QPainter, or an embedded web view) is undecided; these tokens are the source for either, and their flat, square, unshadowed form is chosen to survive a QSS translation without loss.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One giant score numeral with its grade letter boxed in gold.
- One solid plate per screen, holding the single fix and its price in points and damage.
- Hairline-ruled modules named by small boxed tracked-caps labels.
- One colour per measure, carried through bars, sparklines, chips and the points breakdown.
- Grade letters always printed beside their colour.
- A highlighter and a Caveat hand for the marks a coach would add: circles, routes, a note.
- Square corners and no drop shadows anywhere.
- Two themes by day and night, Auto by default.

## Colors

Warm, inked and print-like: ivory or walnut-black grounds, one deep plate colour, a metallic rule colour, and a saturated highlighter kept for human marks. All values live in `theme.css` on `html[data-theme][data-mode]`; frontmatter keys are `<theme>-<mode>-<role>`, and every role exists in all four sets.

### Primary
- **Plate** (`plate`; Timeless olive, Classical burgundy): the one solid surface on a screen, holding the fix. `plate-tx` is its text, `plate-mu` its secondary text, `plate-ac` the price figures (points, damage), `plate-ln` the rules and button edge inside it.

### Secondary
- **Gold Leaf** (`gold`): the metallic rule. It boxes the grade letter, frames the fight grid, draws the header rule on the debrief, outlines tags ("preview", "Boss kill") and award chips. Never a fill.

### Tertiary
- **Highlighter** (`hl`, with `hl-note` for hand-lettered text): Timeless amber-orange by day and glowing amber by night; Classical crimson by day and hot rose by night. It draws the review circles and routes, the focus ring, text selection, and the "you" outline on the podium. It means "look here", and nothing else.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`bg`): the page sheet. **Panel** (`panel`): one step lifted, for cells, tabs, cards and the module label. **Rule** (`ln`): every hairline and grid gap. **Track** (`track`): the unfilled part of a bar.
- **Ink** (`tx`), **Second Ink** (`tx2`), **Muted** (`mu`): text in three strengths. `tx` also draws the strong rules: the page header rule, table heads, the 90 target tick, the active tab ring.
- **Rail** (`rail`, `rail-on`, `rail-tx`, `rail-mu`, `rail-ac`): the left navigation column. Timeless keeps it dark walnut in both modes; Classical keeps it pale by day. `rail-ac` colours "score" in the wordmark.
- **Button** (`btn`, `btn-tx`, `btn-ln`): the filled button. Timeless walnut with ivory text (gold edge at night); Classical ivory with burgundy text.

### Grade inks
- `gS`, `gA`, `gB`, `gC`, `gD` (D and F share `gD`): one ink per grade band, re-tuned per mode so each clears 5:1 on the panel.

### Measure inks
- `m-dmg` Damage, `m-cd` Cooldowns / Abilities on time, `m-rot` Rotation, `m-pace` Pacing, `m-surv` Survival, `m-upk` Upkeep, `m-cast` Casting, `m-act` Activity. A measure's ink is its identity across every page.

### Named Rules
**The One Plate Rule.** A screen has exactly one solid `plate` surface, and it holds the fix: "Fix this first" on the debrief, "Recurring fix" on history, "To move up" on group, "Biggest gap" on the fight card. Everything else sits on `bg` or `panel` behind hairlines.

**The Letter Always Shows Rule.** Grade colour is a second channel. The letter (S, A, B, C, D) is always printed beside or inside its ink: in the grid cells, the score box, the legend, the visits table.

**The One Ink Per Measure Rule.** A measure uses its `m-*` ink in every place it appears (effectiveness bar, breakdown bar, sparkline, chip swatch) and no other measure borrows it.

**The Four Sets Rule.** No hex is written outside `theme.css`. A new colour role is added to all four theme-mode sets at once, or it is not added.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Barlow (with sans-serif)
**Hand Font:** Caveat (with cursive), marks only

**Character:** A sporting, timing-sheet pairing: Barlow Condensed sets numbers tall and narrow so a 64-fight grid fits and a score can be read across a room, while Barlow keeps sentences plain. Figures are tabular everywhere (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on body).

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, 150px, 0.8, −0.02em; 126px under 1420px): the run score numeral and the group placing. One per page. The grade letter rides beside it at 56px in a 2px gold box.
- **Headline** (700, 30px, 1.05): the page title in the header (dungeon name, "After each fight").
- **Title** (600, 23px, 1.15; 34px on the history plate): the fix sentence on the plate.
- **Metric** (700, 30px, 1): measure scores, plate prices (32px), podium ranks; condensed, tabular.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.4): sentences and table cells; verdicts at 15px; secondary notes 12–13px in `mu`. Lead paragraphs cap at 68ch.
- **Label** (700, 11px, 0.16em, uppercase): the boxed module label. Rail group labels, table heads and segmented-row labels use the same face at 600, 11px, 0.12–0.14em.
- **Hand** (Caveat 600, 27px, in `hl-note`): the note beside the circled fights ("review these 4"). Never for interface text.

### Named Rules
**The Condensed Numbers Rule.** Every number a player reads at a glance (score, grade, points, fight times, ranks) is set in Barlow Condensed with tabular figures. Barlow is for sentences.

**The Hand Is Rare Rule.** Caveat appears only as an annotation drawn over data, in highlighter colour, at most once or twice per page.

## Layout

A fixed desktop sheet: `body` has a 1280px minimum width, matching the PyQt window it will become. The shell is a two-column grid, a 200px rail and a fluid main column with 22px top, 28px side and 32px bottom padding. The page header runs the width of the main column, title and meta on the left, view tabs on the right, closed by a 1px `tx` rule (gold on the debrief).

The debrief splits into a fluid left column and a fixed 460px right column divided by a 1px `ln` rule with 28px either side. Its hero is a 260px score block beside the fluid plate (214px under 1420px, the one breakpoint). Modules stack vertically, each with 12px vertical padding and a hairline below. Group and history use a fluid lead block beside a fixed 380–420px plate or chart.

Spacing steps are 6, 8, 12, 16, 22 and 28px: 28 between columns, 22 for shell and plate top padding, 12–16 inside modules, 6–8 inside controls and chips. Rows in ruled lists sit tight (3–5px vertical padding) because the rules, not whitespace, separate them.

### Named Rules
**The Unequal Weight Rule.** No grid of equal-sized cards. The first viewport has one dominant element (the score), one plate, and ruled lists; equal cells are reserved for data that is genuinely uniform, like the 8×8 fight grid or the measure sparklines.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat and print-like. Depth is tonal and ruled: `bg` is the sheet, `panel` is one step lifted, the plate is the only solid block, and 1px rules in `ln` or `tx` do the dividing. There are no drop shadows. The only `box-shadow` values are inset lines that act as rules: a 1px inset ring in `tx` for the active tab or pressed toggle, and a 2px inset underline in gold or `m-cd` for award and roast titles.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline Rule.** Separate with a 1px rule before reaching for space, fill or elevation. Strong rules (`tx`, 1–2px) mark the head of a list or a total; quiet rules (`ln`, 1px) separate rows.

## Shapes

Every corner is square (0px); there is no `border-radius` in the build. Containers are rectangles defined by 1px borders or by the grid's 1px gaps showing the `ln` colour through. The boxed module label straddles the top edge of the plate it names (positioned 12px above it). The only curves are the highlighter's hand-drawn ellipses and routes, deliberately slightly rotated so they read as drawn, not rendered.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square (0px), 1px edge in `btn-ln`.
- **Primary:** `btn` fill, `btn-tx` text, Barlow 600 13px, 10px × 16px padding. On the plate it takes `plate-ln` as its edge.
- **Hover / Focus:** brightness 1.08 on hover; focus is the global 2px `hl` outline at 2px offset.
- **Quiet:** the fight card's Dismiss is an underlined Barlow 500 12px text button in `tx2`, `tx` on hover.

### Tabs and segmented toggles
- **Style:** one shared vocabulary. A `panel` strip with a 1px `ln` border, segments divided by 1px `ln` rules, Barlow 500 in `tx2` (tabs 14px, 8px × 14px; toggles 12px, 6px × 10px).
- **Active:** text goes to `tx` at 600 with a 1px inset `tx` ring (`aria-current="page"` or `aria-pressed="true"`). No fill change.
- **Theme toggles:** two segmented groups in the rail under the "Look" label: Classical | Timeless, and Day | Night | Auto.

### Module label
- **Style:** a small box: `panel` fill, 1px `ln` border, Barlow Condensed 700 11px, 0.16em, uppercase, `tx`, 6px × 10px. It is the module's heading ("Effectiveness", "Where your 25 points went", "Podium"), not a decoration above another heading.
- **On the plate:** it straddles the plate's top edge; on group's plate it drops its fill and takes `plate-ac` text with a `plate-ln` border.

### Fix plate (signature)
The one solid block. `plate` fill, 22px × 18px padding, a Title-size fix sentence, the price in `plate-ac` Metric (−10.3 points) with category and damage in `plate-mu`, a primary button ("Show me on the timeline"), and a `plate-ln` rule above the next two fixes, each with its price right-aligned.

### Measure bars
- **Style:** 10px tall, `track` behind, the measure's `m-*` ink as fill, square ends. A 2px `tx` tick at 90% marks the target on every effectiveness bar, overhanging 4px top and bottom.
- **Row:** name (160px), bar, score right-aligned in Metric with a muted "on target" / "below 90" caption, a 12px muted evidence line beneath.
- **Points breakdown:** the same bars, length proportional to points lost, in a ruled three-column list closed by a 2px `tx` total rule ("Earned · 75 of 100").

### Fight grid (signature)
An 8×8 grid of 74px cells with 1px `ln` gaps and a 1px gold frame. Each `panel` cell carries the fight number and clock time (11px muted), the score and grade letter in the grade's ink (21px), and a 42 × 14 sparkline. A 6px `tx` square in the corner marks a boss. The fights to review are circled over the grid in 5px highlighter strokes at 0.8 opacity, joined by 1.8px routes in review order, with a Caveat note.

### Fight card
The overlay card shown over the game about 2s after combat: 380 × 236, `panel` fill, 1px `tx` border, square. Header in tracked caps (fight type) with time; the damage share in Metric with a measure bar and its 90 "clean" tick; a one-sentence verdict at 15px; a small plate band for the biggest gap or death; measure chips with 8px ink swatches; footer with the "dips score" mark and Dismiss.

### Tags and chips
- **Tag:** 1px gold border, gold text, Barlow 600 11px, 0.1em, uppercase, 2px × 6px. Marks "preview" measures and "Boss kill".
- **Chip:** 1px `ln` border, 12px `tx2`, 2px × 7px; dashed for weak, gold border for awards, `m-cd` border for roasts.
- **Illustrative label:** Barlow 500 11px, 0.12em, uppercase, `mu`. Every value that is not real data carries one.

### Navigation (rail)
`rail` column, 200px. Wordmark in Barlow Condensed 700 22px with "score" in `rail-ac`. Section links at 15px, 9px × 20px; current and hover take `rail-on` fill, current at 600. Group labels ("Recent runs", "Look") in 11px tracked caps `rail-mu`. Recent runs list the place left and the result right in Condensed `rail-tx`.

### Motion
One authored entrance per page, all under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`: the score counts up over 700ms (ease-out quartic), bars grow from the left (700ms, `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, 60ms stagger), then the highlighter circles and routes draw (550ms, `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, 260ms stagger, starting at 900ms) and the note fades in. Theme and mode changes crossfade colours over 220ms. With reduced motion everything renders settled.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the theme tokens and check a new surface in all four sets: Timeless and Classical, each by day and night.
- **Do** give each screen one plate, holding the fix and its price in points and damage.
- **Do** print the grade letter next to every grade colour, and a label next to every measure colour.
- **Do** set numbers in Barlow Condensed with tabular figures.
- **Do** separate with 1px rules (`ln` for rows, `tx` for heads and totals) and keep every corner square.
- **Do** keep the 90 target tick on every measure bar.
- **Do** label illustrative values with the small tracked-caps "illustrative" mark.
- **Do** default day or night to Auto and keep the theme toggles in the rail under "Look".

### Don't:
- **Don't** build equal-weight widget walls of same-sized cards; that is the analytics-dashboard look this system refuses.
- **Don't** add a second plate to a screen or use the plate colour as a general accent.
- **Don't** use the highlighter or Caveat for interface chrome; they are for hand marks, focus, selection and "you".
- **Don't** add drop shadows or rounded corners; the only shadows are inset rule lines.
- **Don't** let a measure borrow another measure's ink.
- **Don't** put a tracked-caps label above a heading as a kicker; the module label is the heading.
- **Don't** write a hex value outside the theme token sets.
